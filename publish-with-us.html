Submission

Submit Your Book

Authors may submit a book proposal or completed manuscript for editorial consideration. Submission does not guarantee publication.

Please prepare

  • Author name, affiliation and contact information
  • Proposed title and subject/category
  • Abstract or book description
  • Table of contents
  • Sample chapter(s) or complete manuscript
  • Author CV/profile
  • Declaration regarding originality and third-party materials

Publishing process

Submit → Editorial Assessment → Review/Development → Production → Publication → Distribution

File limit: The current free FormSubmit service supports a combined file size of up to 10 MB per submission. Larger manuscripts can be submitted using a cloud-storage link in the description field.

Manuscript Submission Form

Complete this form and attach your proposal/manuscript. After submission, the editorial team can contact you using the email address provided.
