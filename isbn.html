ISBN Information

ISBN

International Standard Book Number

INSPIRA intends to publish eligible monographic works with ISBNs obtained through the appropriate ISBN process in India. An ISBN identifies a specific monographic publication or edition; it is not a copyright registration.

Publisher and book metadata should be kept consistent across the ISBN record, copyright page and distribution listings.

For official information and application procedures, consult the Government of India's ISBN system.
