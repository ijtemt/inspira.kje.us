About INSPIRA

An Online Academic Publishing House

INSPIRA Academic Publishing is an independent online publishing initiative focused on making high-quality academic and educational content accessible to students, educators, researchers and professionals.

We aim to publish textbooks, reference books, research monographs, edited volumes, case studies, laboratory manuals and other knowledge resources across a wide range of disciplines.
